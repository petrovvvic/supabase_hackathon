307 West 38th Street is a 21-story, 300,000 SF office building located in the bustling Times Square South submarket. The building features floor plates ranging from 7,000 to 20,000 SF, above average ceiling heights (11’ slab to slab), great amounts of natural light on the top floors, a renovated lobby, five passenger elevators, a freight elevator with direct access through a loading dock on 39th Street, and 24/7 access. The Property features retail tenants including an upscale locally-owned coffee shop (Culture Espresso), ghost kitchen (Kitchen United), and bicycle shop (City Bicycle NYC). The Property is only two avenues east of Hudson Yards.

307 West 38th Street is located four blocks from Times Square, ideally situated between Penn Station and the Port Authority Bus Terminal, with convenient access to the Times Square (N, Q, R, W and 1, 2, 3 trains), Bryant Park (B, D, F, M) and Penn Station (A, C, E) subway stations
